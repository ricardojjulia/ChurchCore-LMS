# ADR-2026-013: SCORM/xAPI Enters the Roadmap (Supersedes ADR-2026-006)

**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** PROPOSED (owner direction 2026-09-26; ratified with COUNCIL-2026-042)
**Supersedes:** ADR-2026-006 (SCORM Runtime Deferred to Sprint 4+)
**Deciders:** Ricardo Julia (project owner)
**Tags:** scorm, xapi, interoperability, roadmap

## Context

ADR-2026-006 deferred SCORM runtime support until a customer contract required it. On 2026-09-26 the owner directed that every capability marked ❌ in the MVP and competitive status report be planned, SCORM/xAPI included. Christian schools and denominations buy third-party SCORM compliance content (safeguarding, child-protection and volunteer-screening training), and "no SCORM" rules the product out of those evaluations before a contract can exist.

## Decision

1. Plan SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 (3rd/4th edition) and xAPI as COUNCIL-2026-042, scheduled after the higher-ranked items in `docs/implementation-plan-2026-q4.md`.
2. The existing `scorm` block type (defined in the schema, with no builder or player) becomes the delivery vehicle, so nothing parallel is invented.
3. Packages run sandboxed, from the private storage bucket through signed URLs, with a first-party runtime API shim. There's no third-party runtime service unless the council chooses one through its own ADR.
4. **ADR-2026-007 (revenue sharing) is not affected.**

## Consequences

- The contract trigger in ADR-2026-006 no longer applies. Scheduling is decided by the Q4 plan's priorities.
- Security review of uploaded package content (HTML/JavaScript) is a binding part of COUNCIL-2026-042.
