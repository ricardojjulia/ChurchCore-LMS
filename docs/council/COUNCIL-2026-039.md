# COUNCIL-2026-039: Paid Courses and Course Storefront

**Status:** BLOCKED — owner decision O6 required before the council votes (see "Decision required")
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Council:** Architecture Council, ChurchCore LMS (vote pending)
**Related:** ADR-2026-007 (revenue sharing deferred as a separate product), COUNCIL-2026-027 (public catalog), `src/app/api/stripe/*`, COUNCIL-2026-034 (self-serve)
**Tags:** payments, stripe, storefront, catalog

## Context

Churches and ministries sell some courses: marriage retreats, leadership certificates, conference follow-ups. Course platforms (Thinkific, Teachable, Kajabi) are built around this. ChurchCore LMS has a public catalog and preview (COUNCIL-2026-027) but no way to charge for enrollment. ADR-2026-007 deliberately deferred **revenue sharing**, where the platform takes a cut, as a separate product decision.

## Decision required (owner)

Choose one before the council votes:

- **Option A — Church collects directly (recommended).** Each org connects its own Stripe account (Stripe Connect, Standard accounts). Buyers pay the church, and ChurchCore takes **no application fee**. This keeps ADR-2026-007 intact: no revenue sharing, and the org stays the merchant of record.
- **Option B — Platform fee.** Same as A, plus a per-transaction application fee. **This supersedes ADR-2026-007** and needs product, legal and tax review first.

## Decision (assuming Option A)

1. Org admins connect Stripe at `/admin/billing/payments` using Stripe Connect Standard OAuth.
2. A course gets a price (one-time; currency per org), an optional coupon code, and an optional seat limit. Free courses are unchanged.
3. **Public catalog "Buy" flow:** the course detail page → Stripe Checkout (on the connected account) → the webhook confirms payment → `enrollCore()` enrolls the buyer (creating their account through the `/join` rules if needed) → a receipt email. Refunds in Stripe unenroll the buyer, after admin confirmation.
4. **Purchases and refunds are recorded** in `course_purchases` and shown in admin reporting.

## Amendments Adopted

Proposed; binding once voted.

1. **Payment is confirmed only by webhook**, never by the redirect back from checkout. Webhook signatures are verified per connected account, and processing is idempotent (the existing `platform_audit_log` pattern).
2. Enrollment still goes through `enrollCore()` with all its gates: prerequisites, age, level and enrollment type. If a gate would block a paid enrollment, the price is refunded automatically.
3. Card data never reaches the application (Stripe Checkout only). No payment objects are logged raw (CLAUDE.md).
4. The `is_synthetic` tenant guard extends to Connect: synthetic orgs can't connect Stripe or sell.
