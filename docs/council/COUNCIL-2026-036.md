# COUNCIL-2026-036: Rubric Grading

**Status:** DRAFT — pending council vote and owner approval
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Council:** Architecture Council, ChurchCore LMS (vote pending)
**Related:** COUNCIL-2026-030 (gradebook grid), `gradeSubmission()` / `setGradeCell()`, `block_submissions`
**Tags:** assessment, grading, teachers, schools

## Context

Teachers grade assignments and discussions with a single score and free-text feedback. Schools, Bible institutes and seminaries expect rubrics: named criteria with performance levels, shared with students before they submit and applied consistently. Every general LMS has them, and they are a checkbox in school evaluations.

## Decision

1. **Rubric model:**
   - A rubric is a reusable, org-owned set of criteria. Each criterion has a title, description and ordered levels (label, points, description).
   - Rubrics are created at `/admin/rubrics` (admins and managers) or inline from the assignment builder (teachers, scoped to their courses).
2. **Attach** a rubric to assignment and discussion blocks. Students see it on the block before submitting.
3. **Grade with the rubric** in submission review and in a side panel of the gradebook grid. The teacher picks a level per criterion, the score is computed (sum, optionally scaled to the block's max points), and comments are per criterion. A manual override of the total is allowed but recorded.
4. **Students see their rubric result**, including the level chosen and comments, on their submission and in the student report.
5. **Reporting:** a per-criterion average across a course in the instructor report, showing where the class struggles.

## Amendments Adopted

Proposed; binding once voted.

1. **Snapshots.** A rubric attached to a block is snapshotted when the first submission is graded. Editing the rubric afterwards doesn't change existing grades; a new version applies to later grading only.
2. Grading through a rubric uses the same authorization as `gradeSubmission()`, including the course-ownership check for teachers (COUNCIL-2026-030). No separate permission path.
3. Rubric scores flow into the existing grade fields, so guardian views, reports, certificates and the gradebook need no parallel logic.
