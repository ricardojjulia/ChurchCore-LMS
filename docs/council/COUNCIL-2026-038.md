# COUNCIL-2026-038: ChurchCore Connect — ChMS Integration with ChurchCore

**Status:** DRAFT — pending council vote and owner approval
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Council:** Architecture Council, ChurchCore LMS (vote pending)
**Related:** ADR-2026-012 (ChurchCore is the ChMS target), COUNCIL-2026-037 (SSO, ChurchCore sign-in), OneRoster receiver (`src/lib/oneroster/*`), `docs/implementation-plan-2026-q4.md` (Epic E)
**Tags:** integration, chms, churchcore, sync, identity, product-family

## Context

Churches keep their people in a ChMS. Today an LMS admin retypes members, households and groups by hand (or bulk-imports a CSV), and course completions never reach the pastor's view of a member. The MVP and competitive status report lists "ChMS integration" as a gap; ADR-2026-012 makes **ChurchCore** (the product family's ChMS) the integration target.

What ChurchCore has (read-only research of `ricardojjulia/ChurchCore` @ `735ae08`, 2026-09-26):

| ChurchCore | Shape | LMS counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| `churches`, `tenants` (control plane, separate DB) | one church per tenant | `organizations` |
| `profiles` (+ `phone`, `date_of_birth`), `church_memberships.role` ∈ `church_admin, pastor, ministry_leader, secretary, member` | people and roles | `profiles`, `profile_roles` |
| `families` | households | `guardian_links` (adult ↔ child) |
| `groups`, `group_members`, `discipleship_groups` | small groups | `section_groups` / cohorts |
| `ministries`, `profile_ministries`, `ministry_tracks` | ministry involvement and pathways | cohorts, learning paths |
| `onboarding_templates` / `_steps` / `_instances` | "next steps" for new members | auto-enrollment, learning paths |
| `education_courses`, `education_enrollments` | a thin course catalog | **superseded by the LMS** (ADR-2026-012) |
| `events`, `attendance` | calendar and attendance | `calendar_events`, attendance blocks |

ChurchCore has **no outbound integration API today**. Its `app/api` routes are internal, cron jobs and inbound provider webhooks. The LMS already has a signed-delivery receiver (`x-churchcore-key-id`, Ed25519, delivery ID, timestamp and staleness checks in `src/lib/oneroster/signature.ts`), originally built for ChurchCore Academy's OneRoster feed.

## Decision

Build **ChurchCore Connect**: a per-tenant, signed, bidirectional sync between one ChurchCore church and one LMS organization, plus ChurchCore sign-in.

1. **Connection.**
   - An LMS org admin creates a connection at `/admin/integrations/churchcore` by entering the ChurchCore church's connect URL and public key, and receives the LMS public key to paste into ChurchCore.
   - The pairing is confirmed with a signed handshake in both directions.
   - One active connection per org. Revoking a connection stops both directions immediately.
2. **Inbound to the LMS (ChurchCore → LMS), full snapshot plus deltas:**
   - people, with name, email, phone, date of birth only where needed for the age gate, and preferred language;
   - church role, mapped `church_admin → admin`, `pastor → manager`, `ministry_leader → teacher`, `secretary → manager` (read-mostly), `member → student`. A child in a family becomes a `student` with `guardian_links` to the family's adults. An admin can override the mapping per person;
   - families (as guardian links), groups and ministries (as LMS cohorts tagged `source='churchcore'`);
   - onboarding templates (as candidate learning paths or auto-enroll rules, chosen per template by the admin).
3. **Outbound from the LMS (LMS → ChurchCore), events:**
   - enrollment created, progress milestones (25/50/75/100 %), course completed, certificate issued (with its public `/verify` link), and path completed;
   - attendance recorded on LMS attendance blocks, if the org enables it.

   ChurchCore shows these on the member profile and in pastor dashboards, and can drive its workflows (for example, completing "Membership 101" advances an onboarding step).
4. **Staged apply, never blind writes.** Inbound deliveries land in staging (the same model as OneRoster: stage → preview → apply), with a per-entity diff. The first sync always needs admin review. Later deltas auto-apply when the org enables it, unless a delivery would deactivate more than N people or change any admin role; those wait for review.
5. **Identity.**
   - Links are `churchcore_person_id ↔ lms profile uid`, stored in the existing `external_entity_links` table with `source_system='churchcore'`.
   - Existing LMS users are matched by verified email only with admin confirmation.
   - People created in the LMS by the sync get accounts they claim through ChurchCore sign-in (COUNCIL-2026-037) or an invite. No passwords are generated or emailed.
6. **Ownership rules.**
   - Fields owned by ChurchCore are read-only in the LMS for linked people (the profile form shows "managed in ChurchCore").
   - Course data is owned by the LMS and never overwritten by ChurchCore.
   - A person removed in ChurchCore is **deactivated** in the LMS: their history is kept and they can no longer sign in to that org. They are never deleted.
7. **Retire the duplicate.** ChurchCore's `education_courses` / `education_enrollments` are replaced by synced LMS data, following ChurchCore's own council process (companion plan below).

## Amendments Adopted

Proposed; binding once voted.

1. **No shared database, and no service-role keys cross products.** All exchange is signed HTTPS between the two apps. Each side stores only the other's public key.
2. **Signatures are Ed25519 with key IDs and rotation, a 5-minute staleness window, and delivery-ID idempotency.** The existing verifier is reused, not forked.
3. **Children's data is minimized.** For people under 18, only name, the family link and (optionally) date of birth are synced. No phone or email is synced for minors unless ChurchCore marks the guardian consent flag. `children_sensitive_data` and `profile_sensitive_fields` are never sent.
4. **Pastoral and care data never crosses:** no `pastoral_notes`, `elder_notes`, `care_assignments`, prayer requests or `ai_*` tables. There's an allow-list of fields per entity; anything not on it is dropped at the sender and rejected at the receiver.
5. **Outbound events contain no grades**, only completion status and certificate numbers, unless the org explicitly enables "share grades with ChurchCore". Grades are educational records, as with `/verify`.
6. **Admin-role changes arriving from ChurchCore always need manual review.** Privilege changes never auto-apply.
7. **Every delivery and apply is audit-logged** with actor, counts and diff hash, and is visible in `/admin/integrations/churchcore`.
8. **Failed outbound deliveries** retry with backoff and then dead-letter, following the guardian-queue pattern (ADR-2026-010). Nothing is dropped silently.

## Implementation Mandate

- **Schema (LMS).** New tables, all with RLS via `current_user_org_id()` and admin or platform-admin policies:
  - `churchcore_connections` (org_id, church_ref, connect_url, their_public_key, their_key_id, our_key_id, status, auto_apply, created_by);
  - `churchcore_deliveries` (inbound staging, the same shape as the OneRoster staging tables);
  - `churchcore_outbound_events` (queue with attempt_count, next_retry_at, failed_at).

  `external_entity_links` is reused with `source_system='churchcore'` plus a nullable `churchcore_connection_id` (its `connection_id` references `oneroster_connections`); its `object_type` check gains `'family'`, `'group'` and `'ministry'`. The LMS private signing key lives in an environment secret (`CHURCHCORE_CONNECT_SIGNING_KEY`), never in the database.
- **Routes.**
  - `POST /api/integrations/churchcore/deliveries` (signed; the inbound receiver);
  - `GET/PUT /api/integrations/churchcore/connection`, `POST /api/integrations/churchcore/deliveries/[id]/preview`, `POST /api/integrations/churchcore/deliveries/[id]/apply` (admin, manager);
  - an Edge Function `churchcore-outbound` (cron, `rejectUnlessCron`) that drains the outbound queue.
- **Triggers.** Enrollment, completion, certificate and path-completion inserts enqueue outbound events through `AFTER INSERT` triggers on the existing tables. This happens in the same transaction, so nothing is lost.
- **UI.** `/admin/integrations/churchcore` has three parts: connection setup, sync status, and a review queue with per-entity diffs. Linked profiles show a "Managed in ChurchCore" badge.
- **Payload contract.** It is versioned (`churchcore-connect/v1`) and documented in `docs/integrations/churchcore-connect-v1.md`, with JSON Schemas for each entity and event. Both repositories test against the same fixture files, the same approach as `academy-contract.test.ts`.

## ChurchCore-side scope (companion plan; built in the ChurchCore repo)

This part is built in the ChurchCore repository, under its council, factory, signed-commit and test-surface rules:

1. **Connect settings.** An LMS connection page for church admins: key generation, pairing and revocation. The private key is held as a tenant secret in the tenant backend, not the control plane.
2. **Sender.** Full-snapshot and delta deliveries built from `profiles`, `church_memberships`, `families`, `groups`, `group_members`, `ministries` and onboarding templates, with an allow-list serializer and the minors rules from Amendment 3.
3. **Receiver** for LMS events: a signed endpoint that stores completions and certificates on the member record. These replace `education_enrollments` rows and can drive `workflows` (for example, advancing onboarding steps).
4. **OIDC provider**, so members sign into the LMS with their ChurchCore account (see COUNCIL-2026-037 for the LMS side).
5. **Retire `education_courses` / `education_enrollments`** after migration: existing rows are exported and offered to the LMS as courses and historical completions.
