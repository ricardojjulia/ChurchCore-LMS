# COUNCIL-2026-041: Hosted Video (Upload and Adaptive Streaming)

**Status:** DRAFT — pending council vote and owner approval (vendor choice needs an ADR)
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Council:** Architecture Council, ChurchCore LMS (vote pending)
**Related:** `src/components/learning/VideoPlayer.tsx` (YouTube/Vimeo embeds today), the `video_stream` block, `must_view` progress, `src/lib/storage.ts`
**Tags:** video, media, storage, vendor

## Context

Video blocks embed YouTube or Vimeo URLs. Churches often can't or won't publish teaching videos publicly: members-only classes, recorded counseling courses, minors on camera. Course platforms host video, and so do most church platforms. `must_view` completion also can't be trusted with third-party embeds.

## Decision

1. **A managed video provider**, not self-hosted transcoding. The vendor (Mux or Cloudflare Stream) is chosen in **ADR-2026-015**, based on: signed playback URLs, per-view analytics, captions, cost per minute stored and streamed, and a data-processing agreement.
2. **Upload in the builder.** A direct upload from the browser to the provider (a resumable upload URL issued by our API) with transcode status shown. YouTube and Vimeo embeds stay supported.
3. **Playback** uses signed, short-lived tokens issued only to users allowed to view the block (the same checks as the learning shell). A watch-progress heartbeat feeds `must_view` completion reliably.
4. **Captions:** upload WebVTT, or use provider auto-captions (en/es).
5. **Plan limits:** storage and streaming minutes per plan in `PLAN_FEATURES`, with usage shown in `/admin/billing`.

## Amendments Adopted

Proposed; binding once voted.

1. Videos are never public: playback always needs a signed token scoped to the org and the viewer, like the private `content-images` bucket rule.
2. Provider API secrets and signing keys are server-side only. Upload URLs are single-use and org-scoped.
3. Deleting a block or course deletes the provider asset, through a queued job with retry.
4. New dependency: the provider's SDK. It's mentioned in the PR and doesn't enter the edge runtime.
