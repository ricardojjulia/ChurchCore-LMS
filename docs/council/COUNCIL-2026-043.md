# COUNCIL-2026-043: Starter Content Library and Course Templates

**Status:** BLOCKED — owner decision O7 required before the council votes (see "Decision required")
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Council:** Architecture Council, ChurchCore LMS (vote pending)
**Related:** AI outline generator (COUNCIL-2026-014), learning paths (COUNCIL-2026-029), `courses`, `course_blocks`
**Tags:** content, templates, library, growth

## Context

Church buyers often choose a platform for its ready-made content. ChurchCore LMS starts every church with an empty catalog (plus the AI outline generator). A starter library shortens time to value from weeks to minutes, and it's what church-focused competitors sell.

## Decision required (owner)

Where the content comes from:

- **(a)** first-party courses written by ChurchCore (with AI drafts reviewed by a theologian or editor);
- **(b)** a licensing partnership with a curriculum publisher;
- **(c)** both.

Licensing affects whether library courses can be copied and edited, or only used as they are.

## Decision (platform capability; content-source agnostic)

1. **A platform-owned library.** `library_courses` are maintained by platform admins in a platform-only org and published as versioned templates: title, description, blocks, quizzes, question banks, and the tags doctrine-neutral, denomination and audience.
2. **Browse and adopt.** Org admins browse `/admin/library` (filter by topic, audience and language) and **adopt** a template. Depending on the license:
   - *copy* makes an org-owned, editable course;
   - *linked* makes a read-only course that receives library updates.
3. **Starter packs.** Curated bundles ("New Members Class", "Discipleship Basics", "Volunteer Onboarding", "Child Safety Essentials") adopt as a learning path plus its courses in one step.
4. **Self-serve signup** (COUNCIL-2026-034) offers a starter pack during onboarding.

## Amendments Adopted

Proposed; binding once voted.

1. Adoption copies content into the org, so learner data stays in the org's tenant. Nothing about learners flows back to the library.
2. Linked (non-editable) courses are enforced in the database, not only hidden in the UI.
3. Each library item records its license and attribution, and shows it on adopted courses.
4. Library content passes the same accessibility and content checks as the product (the page sweep covers the library pages).

## Test Surfaces

- `page:/admin/library`, `page:/platform/library`: routes-table rows; `flows/library.spec.ts` (platform publishes a template → an org adopts it as a copy and as linked → a linked update propagates → a copy stays independent).
- `action:library.adoptTemplate`, `action:library.publishTemplate`: covered by that flow plus unit tests.
- pgTAP: library tables writable only by platform admins; linked courses read-only for the org.
